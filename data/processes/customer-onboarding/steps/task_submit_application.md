## Overview

The customer completes the online application form via the portal. All mandatory fields must be validated before submission is accepted.

## Steps

1. Customer navigates to the portal at `portal.company.com`
2. Completes all mandatory fields (name, address, ID type, contact details)
3. Uploads supporting documents (photo ID, proof of address)
4. Reviews and accepts terms and conditions
5. Submits the application — a confirmation email is sent automatically

## Required documents

| Document | Accepted formats |
|----------|-----------------|
| Photo ID | Passport, driver's licence |
| Proof of address | Utility bill (< 3 months), bank statement |

## Common issues

- **Incomplete upload** — remind customer that all documents must be <5 MB and in PDF/JPG format
- **Address mismatch** — if address differs from ID, request a secondary proof
