## Objective

Onboard new customers from application to active account within 3 business days.

## Performance measures

- Completion < 3 business days
- Error rate < 2%
- Customer satisfaction score > 4.5/5

## Triggers

- New application submitted via portal
- Manual referral from the sales team

## Outcomes

- Customer account is active and accessible via the portal
- All downstream systems (CRM, billing, portal) are provisioned
- Customer has received their welcome email and temporary credentials
- Relationship manager has been notified
