## Objective

Verify the identity of applicants against government-issued documentation and screen for fraud, PEP, and sanctions risks before account activation.

## Performance measures

- Automated check completion < 30 minutes
- Manual review escalation rate < 10%
- False positive rate < 1%

## Triggers

- Identity documents received from customer onboarding step 2
- Manual referral from onboarding team for high-risk applicants

## Outcomes

- Applicant identity confirmed and verification status recorded in CRM
- Onboarding workflow notified to proceed (approved) or halt (rejected)
- Document images archived per 7-year retention policy
- Applicant notified of outcome via email
