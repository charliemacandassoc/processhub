## Overview

Applicant is screened against internal fraud watchlists, external credit bureaus, PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) lists, and sanctions databases.

## Checks performed

| Check | Source | Action on hit |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| Internal fraud list | Internal DB | Auto-reject |
| PEP screening | Dow Jones | Escalate to compliance |
| Sanctions | OFAC / UN | Auto-reject |
| Adverse media | LexisNexis | Escalate to compliance |

## SLA

All checks must complete within **15 minutes** of document receipt.
