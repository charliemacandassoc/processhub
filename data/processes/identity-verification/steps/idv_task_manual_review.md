## Overview

Cases that fail automated OCR checks are reviewed by a compliance analyst within 4 business hours.

## Review steps

1. Open case in the compliance case management system
2. Review original document images alongside OCR output
3. Manually verify name, DOB, document number, and expiry
4. Record decision (approve / reject) with reason code
5. Notify onboarding team of outcome via case system

## Escalation

Cases involving PEP hits or sanctions matches must be reviewed by the Head of Compliance before a decision is recorded.
